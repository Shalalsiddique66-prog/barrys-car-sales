Privacy notice

This notice explains how Barry's Car Sales uses the personal information you give us through the enquiry form.

What we collect

Your name and email address, and, if you choose to give them, your telephone number, the vehicle you are interested in, your message and your preferred contact method.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us.
  • To send you offers and updates, only if you tick the optional box. Our lawful basis is your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for no longer than 12 months after your last contact with us, unless you buy a vehicle from us and we must keep records for legal reasons.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete your information, and object to how we use it. To do so, call 01707 111 222 or contact us using the enquiry form. If you are unhappy with our response you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or tracking.
